A little about me

The person behind the classroom.

My background combines psychology, special education, teaching in France and international classrooms in Bangkok and Singapore. These experiences have made me attentive to different starting points, while reinforcing my belief in clear structure, thoughtful planning and high expectations.

I enjoy creating learning experiences in which French has a real purpose — telling a story, preparing a performance, discovering another culture or contributing to a wider school event. I want students to understand what they are learning, see their own progress and, above all, develop the desire to keep learning.

Selected Practice

The choices behind the learning.
French with a purpose.

01

Shadow Theatre

Language through storytelling.

Students explored French stories before selecting one to bring to the stage. Vocabulary, oral expression, music and sound effects became part of a shared final production.

Oral language · Storytelling · Collaboration

Explore project — Shadow Theatre

Learning intention

Use French vocabulary and oral expression to bring a chosen story to life.

From story to stage

Discover and choose a story, develop its vocabulary and oral expression, add music and sound effects, then present the production.

Instructional decisions

Student choice gives the language work a shared purpose. Vocabulary and voice become tools for communicating a story, alongside music and sound.

Assessment

The final production served as the assessment, bringing the language work together in a performance.

02

Francophonie

Taking French beyond the classroom.

A school-community initiative designed to make French visible across the school and create opportunities to encounter Francophone language and culture.

Culture · Community · Authentic purpose

Explore project — Francophonie

Purpose

Give the French language and Francophone cultures a place in the wider school community, beyond individual lessons.

My contribution

I organise Francophonie celebrations and events that promote the French language throughout the school community.

Related cultural projects

To celebrate Francophonie and promote French across the school, I also organised an exhibition on French-speaking countries, a choir singing French songs, and a QR-code treasure hunt featuring fun facts about French-speaking countries.

03

La Chandeleur

A shared celebration with a purpose.

Students across age groups and grade levels collaborated to prepare, promote and sell crêpes, sharing a French tradition while raising funds for charity.

Collaboration · Culture · Fundraising

Explore project — La Chandeleur

Purpose

Bring La Chandeleur into the school community through a crêpe fundraiser, connecting a French cultural celebration with a shared charitable purpose.

From preparation to sale

Students took part in preparing crêpes, creating promotional materials and selling them. The celebration became a collaborative project, with a real audience and a purpose beyond the classroom.

Across ages and grade levels

The project brought students of different ages and grade levels together to contribute to the same event. Collaboration connected the preparation, promotion and sale of the crêpes.

Culture in the school community

Sharing the celebration gave students a practical way to introduce others to a French tradition while contributing to a fundraising effort.
